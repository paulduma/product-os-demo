# Level 5 — Director

**Goal: delegate, don't execute.**

> Roadmap : [index](README.md) · Niveau 5/5 · Précédent → [Level 4 — Operator](04-operator.md)

---

## What to do

The final shift: from running skills yourself to supervising agents that run multi-step work on your behalf. Your job becomes deciding and validating, not doing. This is the level almost nobody starts at — and that's the point of the roadmap: you don't get to skip to "I have agents working for me" without having done the work that makes you able to tell whether what they produce is any good.

## You're done with this level when

You're spending more time reviewing agent output than producing your own.
