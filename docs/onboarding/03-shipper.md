# Level 3 — Shipper

**Goal: close the loop, alone, end to end.**

## What to do

Take a spec's output and turn it into a ticket yourself. Implement it. Open a pull request. Merge it to GitHub. This is the level where "using AI" stops being about reading good output and starts being about *shipping* — you're now responsible for something that exists in production, or at least in a real repo.

## Why it's the hard one

This is deliberately the hardest jump in the roadmap. Levels 1 and 2 are about trust in the tool; level 3 is about trust in yourself using it on something that counts.

## You're done with this level when

You have a merged PR that started as a skill's output.
